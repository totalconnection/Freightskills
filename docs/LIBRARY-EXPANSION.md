## SEO approach and evidence

Each article has one reader question, a unique title and description, a stable canonical path, source video attribution, relevant topic navigation and related articles. Overlapping lessons share a URL. A searchable /videos directory connects every source video to its guide. Article schema lists every source video; upload dates and VideoObject data are not invented. Source thumbnails load lazily; video players load on request. The article-search payload excludes article bodies.

A current qualitative search-result check for shipper prospecting, quoting, carrier qualification and broker/agent/dispatcher comparisons supports separating practical how-to intent from role/compliance comparisons. This is search-result observation and editorial judgment, not measured demand, keyword difficulty or traffic forecasting. No Analytics or Search Console data was available. No search-volume figures or ranking guarantees are used. Competitor content is not cited or reproduced in the public articles.
