# SEO and topic plan

## Evidence boundary

Repository findings are observed: explicit global noindex in the old draft; no sitemap/robots route files; no canonical setup or Article schema; three hard-coded article drafts; five existing resource paths. Existing local source archive contains 66 public YouTube source entries and 33 course transcripts. Source counts do not establish current channel inventory, traffic, authority, or search demand.

No analytics or Search Console data was available in inspected project files. Search results were sampled for freight quotation, carrier qualification, LTL, and drayage. They indicate a mixture of practical how-to questions and introductory explanations, but do not establish search volume, ranking opportunity, or conversion value. Topic priority below is an editorial inference based on the user's scope and the depth of their available lessons. No competitor sources or references are included in published article copy.

## Implemented foundation

Per-page titles/descriptions; production-domain canonicals; social preview metadata; semantic navigation/headings; accurate basic Article schema with isBasedOn video source; server-rendered article bodies; topic and related-article links; real 404 handling; staged noindex/nofollow; sitemap empty until production approval and excluding unapproved articles thereafter. VideoObject is intentionally omitted pending reliable video dates and media metadata. Image dimensions, local WebP artwork and click-to-load video reduce unnecessary transfer and third-party embed cost. No ranking or Core Web Vitals claims.

Google reference: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/intro — robots blocking alone does not prevent indexing. Preview is local; metadata prevents indexing if the same build is placed on an accessible review host. Production needs independent deployed-header and HTML checks.
